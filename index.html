Social Studies Educational Website Proposal

Social Studies Explorers Hub: An Interactive Learning Platform for Grade 8 World History

I. Rationale

In today’s digital era, social media platforms such as YouTube and TikTok play a significant role in students’ learning experiences. Based on the findings of the study “The Effects of Using Social Media in Learning Achievements of Grade 8 Learners in Social Studies,” social media enhances academic performance, engagement, and motivation.

Definition of Terms

  • Social Media: Digital platforms that allow users to create, share, and interact with content.
  • Learning Achievement: The measurable academic performance of students.
  • Engagement: The level of participation and involvement in learning activities.
  • Motivation: The internal drive that encourages students to learn and participate.
  • Interactive Learning: A method of teaching that actively involves students through activities and participation.

II. Objectives

  • Improve learning achievement in World History.
  • Provide engaging video lessons.
  • Encourage participation through interactive games and quizzes.
  • Reinforce learning through feedback-based activities.
  • Promote interest using trivia and fun facts.

III. Website Features

1) Homepage

  • Introduction and welcome video.
  • Easy navigation.

2) Video Lessons Page

Embedded YouTube and TikTok videos organized by topics:

  • Imperialism and Colonialism
  • World War I
  • World War II
  • Cold War

3) Interactive Games and Quizzes

  • Interactive Games: Digital activities designed to enhance learning through play.
  • Quizzes: Assessment tools used to measure understanding.

Types include:

  • Multiple Choice Quiz
  • True or False
  • Matching Type
  • Timeline Game
  • Map Identification Game

Core features include instant feedback, score display, and explanations of answers.

4) Trivia Corner

  • Trivia: Interesting facts used to increase curiosity and engagement.
  • Includes “Did You Know?” facts.

5) About Us Page

  • Research background
  • Purpose
  • Researchers’ profile

IV. Website Structure

  • Home
  • Video Lessons
  • Games and Quizzes
  • Trivia Corner
  • About Us

V. Innovation

  • Combines social media and education.
  • Uses gamification.
  • Promotes active learning.

Gamification: The application of game elements in non-game contexts to improve engagement.

VI. Expected Outcomes

  • Improved Academic Performance: Students will demonstrate measurable gains in World History mastery, targeting a 20-25% increase in quiz scores and content retention through interactive video lessons, gamified assessments, and instant feedback.
  • Increased Student Motivation: The platform aims to boost intrinsic motivation by 30% (measured via pre/post surveys) through familiar social media formats, badges, leaderboards, and weekly trivia challenges.
  • Enhanced Engagement Levels: Interactive features target 80%+ lesson completion rates and higher time-on-task through timeline games, map identification, and collaborative quiz challenges.
  • Strengthened Critical Thinking: Learners will develop evidence-based reasoning through primary source analysis, branching quizzes, and discussion prompts connecting history to modern Philippine contexts.

VII. Conclusion

The Social Studies Explorers Hub presents a transformative approach to Grade 8 World History learning by integrating popular social media formats with interactive educational design. Through gamified quizzes, timeline challenges, map activities, and AI-enhanced trivia, the platform improves achievement, engagement, and motivation while supporting DepEd digital learning priorities for 2026.

With mobile-first accessibility, multilingual support (English and Tagalog), offline download capability, and teacher dashboards for monitoring progress, the platform provides a scalable and sustainable model for technology-driven Social Studies education in diverse Philippine school settings.

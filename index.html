About Us

"Explore the Past, Engage the Present, Learn for the Future"

Hi! We are third-year students from Batangas State University ARASOF Nasugbu Campus, majoring in Social Studies. This website is part of our research on how social media improves student learning in Social Studies through engaging and technology-driven approaches.

What This Platform Offers

  • Watch video lessons
  • Play interactive games
  • Answer quizzes with feedback
  • Explore history trivia

Website Proposal Highlights

  • Rationale: Social media enhances engagement, motivation, and performance.
  • Objectives: Improve World History achievement through interactive learning.
  • Innovation: Gamification + social media content for active participation.
  • Expected Outcomes: Better scores, higher motivation, stronger critical thinking.
  • Conclusion: A scalable, student-friendly, and technology-driven history platform.
